# APRENDOlab

## Proof of Concept dApps where we explore how local challenges from Latam communities could be tackled using Ethereum.

### Program Overview

**#APRENDOlab** is an innovative follow-up program to **#APRENDOcripto**, designed to deepen participants' understanding of blockchain technology while addressing real-world challenges. Over the course of a month-long, in-person workshop series, participants from communities acrorss LATAM will collaborate with the **APRENDO team** to co-create a decentralized **Ethereum application (dApp)** as an educational tool. The program empowers graduates to identify pressing local problems, design practical solutions, and gain hands-on experience in the creation process of decentralized applications.

## Objectives

#APRENDOlab focuses on empowering individuals and communities while showcasing Ethereum's transformative potential in local contexts.

- **Test Ethereum Use Cases in Local Latin American Vulnerable Communities**: Facilitate the experimentation and testing of Ethereum-based solutions to address specific local needs, such as financial inclusion, governance, or resource allocation.
- **Enhance Practical Knowledge**: Provide hands-on experience in designing and testing decentralized applications (dApps) for real-world use cases using Ethereum.
- **Empower Communities**: Equip participants with advanced blockchain skills tailored to their local challenges, enabling them to explore and locate meaningful solutions that resonate with their unique socio-economic contexts.

## Program Structure

Each #APRENDOlab program consists of four in-person interactive workshops, conducted weekly over the course of a month, following the completion of **#APRENDOcripto**. The program begins by introducing participants to blockchain technology’s role in global challenges, with case study analyses and discussions on its applications in finance, governance, and resource management. Participants then identify local issues and collaborate to design a decentralized application (dApp) that addresses a pressing challenge in their community. After the initial brainstorming and conceptualization phase, the APRENDO technical team, led by a Full Stack dApp developer, translates the prototype into a functional proof-of-concept (PoC) dApp based on the Scaffold-ETH2 toolkit. The next phase involves hands-on testing of the dApp, where participants engage with the prototype in simulated real-world scenarios, providing feedback for refinement. Finally, participants present their solutions to grassroots leaders, local organizations, and stakeholders, opening opportunities for potential real-world adoption and further development.

## Deliverables

### Functional Prototype
The resulting **dApp from each APRENDOlab edition** will be uploaded to this repository under an **open-source license**. These repositories are intended as educational resources for other projects and Ethereum developers.

### Impact Report
Each **APRENDOlab edition** will generate an individual **impact report**. This report will compile feedback and perspectives from grassroots organizations, educators, developers, and participants, highlighting **key lessons learned, successful strategies, and best practices** to ensure **scalability and replicability** in future editions.

### Prototyping Framework and Standardized Architecture
To streamline **dApp development**, we leverage **ScaffoldETH**, a powerful toolkit known for its **rapid prototyping capabilities** and fully integrated **front-end and back-end infrastructure**. This enables quick iteration, testing, and deployment for **Proof of Concept (PoC) dApps**.

Additionally, instructors guide participants in designing **dApps** using proven and simplified **smart contract patterns** to enhance feasibility, such as:
- **Request-Validate-Approve**: A structured approach for verifying transactions or actions.
- **Publish-Subscribe**: A modular event-driven design pattern.
- **Multisignature**: A security-enhancing framework for decentralized decision-making.
- **State Machines**: A structured model for managing contract logic and workflows.
